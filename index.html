ABOUT

About Saddu WhatsApp Scheduler

Saddu WhatsApp Scheduler is a 100% static, client-side web application designed to be deployed for free on GitHub Pages.

  • No Server Required: Operates entirely within your browser using HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, and LocalStorage.
  • No Unofficial Bots: Leverages official WhatsApp Click-to-Chat deep links (https://wa.me/), preventing account bans.
  • Privacy First: Your phone numbers, contacts, and personal messages never leave your device.
